SCANNING AND SOURCE INTEGRITY

The GitHub Actions VM temporarily downloads and unpacks the selected official
DEBs, updates ClamAV and runs the mandatory malware and separate PUA passes.
Malware, scan errors and limits stop delivery; PUA-only matches are reported
separately. The bootstrap contains a scan record bound to the official source
hash. Your computer downloads the same DEB and checks its size and SHA256
before unpacking and building the client. It does not run ClamAV. CI scan
results do not guarantee absence of unknown threats or future vulnerabilities.
